# lowtouch.ai Brand Style Guide for PDF and PNG Generation

Apply the following branding rules to all PDF and PNG outputs. This is the canonical style reference, derived from the official lowtouch.ai platform deck (v53).

## Color Palette

Primary background: Deep navy blue (#0A1128 or very close, dark saturated navy)
Secondary background: Slightly lighter navy for card/container areas (#111B3C approximate)
Accent color 1 (primary brand accent): Hot pink / magenta (#FF00FF or #E930CF range, used for headlines, highlights, decorative lines, and icon backgrounds)
Accent color 2: Bright green (#7FFF00 or #6BEB2D range, used for subheadings, taglines, and secondary callout text)
Text color (primary): White (#FFFFFF) for all body text, feature labels, and headings on dark backgrounds
Text color (secondary): Light gray or muted lavender (#A0A0C0 range) for supporting descriptions and captions
Icon background: Purple-to-pink gradient (a soft purple #9B30FF blending to magenta #E930CF), used as rounded-square card backgrounds behind white icons

## Typography

Headings: Bold sans-serif (closest to Inter, Poppins, or similar geometric sans-serif). Large, high-impact. Use hot pink/magenta for primary headings, white for secondary headings.
Subheadings/taglines: Bright green, semi-bold or bold, slightly smaller than main headings.
Body text: White, regular weight, clean sans-serif. Keep it readable against the dark navy background.
Feature labels: White, bold, slightly larger than body text.
Feature descriptions: Light gray or muted white, regular weight.

## Layout and Visual Patterns

- Full-bleed dark navy backgrounds across all pages/slides. No white or light backgrounds.
- Decorative element: Flowing, curved wireframe lines (thin, pink/magenta, semi-transparent) typically placed on the left side or bottom-left corner. These are abstract, flowing wave/ribbon shapes made of fine parallel curved lines. They add visual energy without overwhelming content.
- Cards and feature blocks use rounded-corner containers with subtle borders or slightly lighter navy fill.
- Icon cards: Rounded square tiles with a purple-to-pink gradient background, containing white line-art icons. These are used for feature callouts (like "No-Code AI Agents," "Private and Secure," etc.)
- Content is generally left-aligned or left-biased for text, with icon cards or visual elements on the right.
- The lowtouch.ai logo sits in the bottom-right corner. The logo is: "lowtouch" in white bold sans-serif + ".ai" where the dot is a magenta/pink circle and "ai" is white.
- Generous whitespace (relative to the dark background) between sections. Do not crowd content.

## Logo Usage

The lowtouch.ai logo mark: "lowtouch" in white, followed by a pink/magenta dot (circle), followed by "ai" in white. Always place on the dark navy background. Typically bottom-right of the page/slide.

## Do's
- Use the dark navy as the dominant canvas. Every page should feel like a continuation of the same visual system.
- Use pink/magenta sparingly but confidently for headlines and decorative elements.
- Use green only for subheadings and taglines, never for body text or backgrounds.
- Keep icon cards consistent (rounded square, gradient purple-to-pink background, white icon).
- Include the flowing wireframe line decoration on at least the cover/first page and closing page.

## Don'ts
- No white or light-colored page backgrounds.
- No em dashes in any text. Use commas, periods, semicolons, or parentheses.
- No generic corporate blues or grays. The palette is specifically deep navy + hot pink + bright green.
- Do not use gradients on text. Gradients are for icon card backgrounds and decorative elements only.
- No busy patterns or stock photo backgrounds. Keep it clean and minimal.
- Do not use any colors outside this palette without explicit approval.

## File Output Notes
- PDFs: Use these styles for all page layouts, headers, footers, section dividers, and data visualizations.
- PNGs: Use the dark navy background, ensure text is crisp at export resolution, and maintain the same color palette and typography hierarchy.
- For charts or data visualizations, use the pink/magenta as the primary data color, green as secondary, and white for labels/axes against the dark navy background.
